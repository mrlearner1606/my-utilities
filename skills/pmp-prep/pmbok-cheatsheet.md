## Common Exam Mix-ups
- **Risk Register** vs **Risk Report**: register = detailed log of individual risks; report = summary for stakeholders on overall risk exposure
- **EVM basics**: PV (planned value), EV (earned value), AC (actual cost), CPI = EV/AC, SPI = EV/PV
- **Servant leadership**: remove blockers, don't command — think Rajini in a mentor role, not the "Superstar" boss role 😄
